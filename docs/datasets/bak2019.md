## Task details

The experiment included three separate sessions comprising 25 trials for each task. Each trial included:
- Introduction period: 2 s
- Task period: 10 s
- Inter-trial break: 17–19 s
Triggers were marked at the start of the task periods.

- Task types: Right-hand finger-tapping (RHT), left-hand finger-tapping (LHT), foot-tapping (FT).
- RHT/LHT: Unilateral complex finger-tapping (thumb to fingers, index to little, then reverse) at 2 Hz.
- FT: Tapping the foot on the dominant hand's side at 1 Hz.
