## Task details

11 participants went through one run of 5-min resting state data collection, and all 12 participants went through three 8-min runs of left- and right-hand ball- squeezing task (illustrated in Fig. 3). During the resting state measurement, we asked participants not to move and to remain mentally idle as much as possible for 5 min. During the ball-squeezing task, the participants sat in front of a computer screen (13.3 in. with resolution of 2560 × 1600) and held one rubber ball (2.5 in. in diameter) in each hand. When the task started, they squeezed the ball in either the left or the right hand according to a visual cue shown on the computer screen accompanied by an audio sound. The stimulus was 5 s, and the rest in between was randomly varied between 5 and 15 s. In each run, there were 15 trials with left-hand stimulus and 15 trials with right-hand stimulus in a fully randomized order. The stimulus presentation was created with PsychoPy software. The probe design was the same layout as in the simulation design [Fig. 1(a)], with two patches covering the motor regions on each hemisphere.
